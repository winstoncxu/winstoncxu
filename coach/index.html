How I talk

      
Paste a few paragraphs of your own speech so answers sound like you: an answer from an old interview, a transcript or voice-memo text, or anything you've written in your natural voice. Your own spoken answers from the practice log are used too. This only shapes the wording, never the facts.

      
      

    

    

      
Your confirmed facts

      
The coach only treats your resume and what you write here as true. Add real details you've confirmed (a baseline metric, the stakeholder's role, what failed), one per line. Practice answers never become facts automatically.
